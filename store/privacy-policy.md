# Privacy policy: Spotify Faster / Slower Control

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Spotify Faster / Slower Control ("the extension") changes the playback speed and pitch of the Spotify web player (open.spotify.com) in your own browser. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.

## What the extension stores

Everything stays on your own computer, in Chrome's extension storage:

- **Your settings**: speed, pitch, tape mode, bass, drum tail, reverb, and whether the extension is switched on.
- **Per-song settings**: for songs you give their own settings (the panel's "This song" tab), the title, artist and album, used to recognise the song, with those settings. At most 2,000 songs are kept. "Use usual" on a song removes it.
- **Recently played**: the title, artist, album, cover image link and Spotify page links of songs you play in the web player, with the time you played them. At most the last 200 songs are kept. You can remove single songs from it.
- **Diagnostics**: the most recent start error and a small technical snapshot (audio levels, speed and pitch values), used only to troubleshoot problems on your own computer.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any other server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.
- It does not record or save audio. The tab's sound is processed live, as it plays, and then discarded.

## Permissions

- **Tab capture**: to take the sound of the Spotify tab and process it (pitch correction, bass, reverb, drum tail) so it plays sped up or slowed down at the original pitch. This only happens after you switch the extension on.
- **Offscreen document**: to run that audio processing in the background.
- **Storage**: to remember your settings, per-song settings and your Recently played list on your computer.
- **Access to open.spotify.com**: to set the playback speed, add the speed button to the player bar, and record and replay your Recently played songs. The extension does not run on any other site.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also remove single songs from the Recently played tab in Spotify's queue panel (right-click a song, then Remove from history).
