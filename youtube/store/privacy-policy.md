# Privacy policy: YouTube Faster / Slower Control

Last updated: 5 November 2026

YouTube Faster / Slower Control ("the extension") changes the playback speed and pitch of videos on YouTube in your own browser, and holds the picture back slightly so it stays in sync with the processed sound. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## What the extension stores

Only your settings (speed, pitch, tape mode, picture sync and its fine-tune, bass, drum tail, reverb), in Chrome's extension storage on your own computer.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any other server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.
- It does not record or save audio or video. The tab's sound and the video picture are processed live, as they play, and then discarded.
- It does not keep any history of what you watch.

## Permissions

- **Tab capture**: to take the YouTube tab's sound and process it (pitch correction, bass, reverb, drum tail) so it plays sped up or slowed down at the original pitch. This only happens after you switch the extension on.
- **Offscreen document**: to run that audio processing in the background.
- **Storage**: to remember your settings on your computer.
- **Access to youtube.com (www, m and music)**: to set the playback speed on the video and show the slightly delayed picture that keeps it in sync with the sound. The extension does not run on any other site.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
